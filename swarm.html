Five aircraft map a six-storey building on their own (54 s)

One recorded run, timelapsed. Five aircraft split the floors between themselves with no pilot per aircraft, hold a relay chain through concrete, carry data out of radio shadow, and all five return and land. Zero crashes, zero human input.

This is PX4-SITL rigid-body simulation — the same autopilot firmware a real aircraft runs. It has not yet flown on hardware, and the first thing we want from hardware is a sim-to-real divergence number, not a demo video.
